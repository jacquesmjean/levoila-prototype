The Public Ledger

Where every dollar goes.

Heritage work only survives on trust. So we show the books. Every dollar raised and every dollar spent, sorted by site and by purpose, updated as the money moves. No summaries you have to take on faith, no numbers behind a login.

How we keep it honest

Two signatures on every payment

No single person can move money. Each disbursement is authorized by two people before it clears, and both names are attached to the record.

A tamper-evident record

Entries are hash-chained. Once a line is written it cannot be quietly edited or deleted. Any change leaves a mark.

Local hands, local records

Work on the ground is done and logged by Haitian teams at each site. Restricted gifts are tracked separately and spent only on what they were given for.
